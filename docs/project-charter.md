# SimplePlans — Project Charter

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** November 25, 2026  
**Project Type:** Public-facing web application  
**Project Status:** Planning

## 1. Problem Statement

Health insurance in the United States is difficult to understand. Many individuals and families lack the knowledge, resources, or confidence to evaluate their healthcare coverage options and understand the financial implications of their decisions.

Whether selecting coverage through an employer or shopping for insurance through HealthCare.gov, consumers are frequently presented with unfamiliar terminology, complicated plan structures, and overwhelming amounts of information.

These barriers make informed healthcare decisions unnecessarily difficult.

## 2. Project Purpose

Develop a free, accessible, and user-friendly web application that helps individuals understand health insurance, compare available healthcare plans, and estimate potential healthcare expenses.

SimplePlans will combine educational resources with interactive financial comparison tools to help users make informed decisions independently.

## 3. Mission Statement

Make health insurance easier to understand and empower people to make informed healthcare decisions, regardless of their education, income, or previous knowledge of insurance.

## 4. Target Audience

- Individuals evaluating employer-sponsored health insurance.
- Individuals shopping for coverage through HealthCare.gov.
- Young adults transitioning off their parents' health insurance.
- Self-employed individuals and independent contractors.
- Families anticipating significant healthcare expenses.
- Individuals with limited healthcare or financial literacy.
- Anyone seeking to better understand their healthcare coverage.

## 5. Core Objectives

- Explain healthcare terminology in plain language.
- Educate users on how the American health insurance system works.
- Help users navigate employer-sponsored and Marketplace insurance options.
- Allow users to manually enter and compare health insurance plans.
- Estimate annual healthcare costs under different circumstances.
- Communicate the financial advantages and disadvantages of each plan.
- Provide educational resources and answers to common healthcare questions.
- Deliver an intuitive, accessible experience across desktop and mobile devices.

## 6. In Scope — Initial Release

- Public-facing homepage.
- Healthcare education center.
- Employer-sponsored insurance education.
- HealthCare.gov and Marketplace insurance education.
- Interactive health insurance plan comparison tool.
- Manual entry of all insurance plan information.
- Healthcare expense scenario simulator.
- Interactive financial calculations and visualizations.
- Searchable healthcare glossary.
- Frequently asked questions section.
- Transparent calculation methodology.
- References to authoritative healthcare resources.
- Responsive and accessible website design.

## 7. Out of Scope — Initial Release

- User accounts and authentication.
- Remote storage of personal information.
- Databases of existing or commonly offered insurance plans.
- Automatic population of insurance plan information.
- Automated insurance quotes.
- Automated Marketplace subsidy eligibility determinations.
- Integration with HealthCare.gov or insurance providers.
- Insurance sales or enrollment.
- Medical records or patient information.
- AI-powered insurance document processing.
- Personalized medical advice.

## 8. Technical Approach

SimplePlans will utilize a client-side architecture. All insurance plan information will be manually entered by the user, and financial calculations will be processed locally within the user's browser.

The application will not require a backend server, database, or user authentication system for its initial release.

Educational content will be maintained within the application's source repository.

## 9. Success Criteria

- A user with no prior knowledge of health insurance can complete a plan comparison without assistance.
- Users can better understand both employer-sponsored and Marketplace insurance options.
- Financial calculations produce accurate results within documented assumptions.
- Users can understand why one plan may be more economical than another.
- Users can easily locate definitions and educational resources.
- The application is responsive and accessible.
- The application does not require personal identifying information or account creation.
- The application is publicly deployed and professionally documented.

## 10. Product Principles

- Simplicity over complexity.
- Education over assumption.
- Transparency over ambiguity.
- Privacy by design.
- Accessibility for everyone.
- Accuracy over convenience.
- Independence from insurance providers.
